## Why
Saved AI image captions do not reach the public gallery. Readers should see them according to their existing AI preferences.

## What Changes
- Publish generated captions separately from author captions.
- Select and label captions consistently in gallery tooltips, alt text and the enlarged viewer.
- Honor both the AI summary preference and mod-page AI visibility.

## Capabilities
### New Capabilities
- `gallery-ai-captions`: Saved image descriptions displayed according to reader preferences.
### Modified Capabilities
None.

## Impact
PublicImage, public data builder, site gallery and image viewer, focused tests. No new LLM calls or changes to image selection.
